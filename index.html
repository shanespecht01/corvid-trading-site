The name

A crow's feather looks black until the light hits it. Then the color no one noticed was there all along.

Corvid Trading Company is named for the crows that visit our home — birds most people write off as plain and loud, when they're some of the smartest, most striking animals around. Their feathers carry blues and violets the human eye can barely catch. That's the whole idea behind what we do: find what's genuinely good and a little overlooked, and put it in front of people at exactly the moment the light hits.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Is there a minimum order?

Most items have a minimum — often around 12–24 for apparel and 50–100 for smaller goods — but we'll always find options that fit your quantity and budget.

How long does it take?

Typical turnaround is about 2–3 weeks after you approve the artwork. Rush options are often available when you're up against a date — just tell us the deadline.

Can you match our brand colors and logo?

Yes. Send your logo and brand colors and we'll send back a proof so you can see exactly how it'll look before anything is produced.

Do I need print-ready artwork?

No. Send whatever you have. If your logo needs cleanup for print or embroidery, we'll handle it as part of the job.

Can I see a sample first?

For larger and apparel orders we can usually get you a physical sample or a digital proof before you commit to the full run.

What area do you serve?

We're based in the Fargo–Moorhead area and work with businesses across the region — and we ship nationwide when a job calls for it.
